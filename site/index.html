Exhaustion-reversal backtest

Your rule, simulated: a doji/exhaustion candle after a same-direction run, entered as an ATM 2-trading-day SPY option, targeting +20% / stopping at -10% of the option's modeled premium. Reported at every multi-timeframe agreement level, since the rule didn't specify how many of the 4 timeframes must confirm. Premiums are Black-Scholes modeled from VIX as an IV proxy, not real historical quotes — no free source for those exists. The sample runs on ~2 years of real 5-minute SPY data; requiring more timeframes to agree shrinks the sample fast, so weight rows with more trades more heavily. Read this as a directional read on the setup, not a precise number.

Out-of-sample check (2026-10-11): splitting the 2-year sample into two 12-month halves and testing every trend-length/doji-width combination on each half separately, every single combination was profitable on the first half and unprofitable on the second — including whichever combination looked best when tuned on just the first half. That's a sign the apparent edge above tracks which period you happen to sample more than it tracks the rule itself. Treat the numbers on this page as "this setup, tested" rather than "a proven edge."

How these are calculated

The levels (lines on the chart, table below) are recalculated once a day at 5:00 AM Central from the last 60-90 days of real BTC and ETH trade data, and held fixed until the next run. The chart itself is live — Kraken's WebSocket streams the forming candle on every trade, independent of the daily levels calculation. A setup is labeled confirmed only after the latest candle closes through the three-part patience gate; it is context to assess, not an automatic buy or sell order:

  • Volume profile (POC / VAH / VAL) — where the most trading volume has happened over the last 60 days. POC is the single busiest price; VAH/VAL bound the zone containing 70% of that volume.
  • Range high / low — the highest and lowest price traded in that same 60-day window.
  • Golden pocket — the 61.8%–65% retracement of the most recent sizeable price swing (8%+), a zone traders commonly watch for reactions.
  • Prior week / month high-low, current week / month open — fixed calendar reference points.
  • Flip level — an old high or low that price has since closed through, so it's now expected to act the opposite way (old resistance holding as support, or vice versa).
  • Confluence — levels within 0.5% of each other are merged into one zone; the more levels agree, the higher the "strength".
  • NEW / ▲▼ badges — compares today's levels to the last snapshot; a level that moved more than 0.1% shows the change, a level that didn't exist yesterday is tagged NEW.

How this is calculated

This is a mechanical backtest of a user-defined rule, not a live signal engine — SPY gets different treatment than BTC/ETH because it's not a level map, it's one specific intraday options setup being tested for odds.

  • Candles — Heikin-Ashi, built from real 5-minute SPY candles (Alpaca's free IEX feed, ~2 years of history) resampled into 10m/15m/20m/30m bars anchored to the 9:30 AM ET open. VIX comes from Yahoo Finance, daily only.
  • Historical setup rule — a run of same-direction HA candles followed by a doji whose rejection-side wick exceeds the full range of at least one of the trend candles before it, during 10:00–12:00 ET.
  • Trade — an ATM option 2 trading days out, direction set by the exhaustion (downtrend exhaustion → call, uptrend → put), priced with Black-Scholes using VIX as an implied-vol proxy since no free source has real SPY options quotes.
  • Exit — first of +20% target, -10% stop, or expiry, tracked on the modeled premium.

Nothing here is a buy or sell signal — it's the measured historical odds of the rule you described, with every modeling assumption stated above it, not hidden in the numbers.
